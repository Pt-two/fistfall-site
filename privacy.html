Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 September 2026

What we collect

Nothing. FistFall has no accounts of its own, no analytics and no advertising, and it sends nothing to us.

What stays on your Vision Pro

Your campaign progress, best scores, the objects you have collected, your workout tally and your settings are saved on this device only.

Your room, your hands

In your own room, FistFall uses the room's layout and your hand positions to place objects and to know when you punch. This is processed on the device while you play and is never stored or sent anywhere.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores are sent to Apple's Game Center to appear on leaderboards. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. To sign out, open Settings › Game Center on your Vision Pro.
