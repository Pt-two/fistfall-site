Who can play

You need an Apple Vision Pro and must meet Apple's own age requirements for it. If you are under 18, get a parent's or guardian's permission first. Shatter Punch is a physical game: younger players should be supervised by an adult who has read these terms.

Your play space

Shatter Punch asks you to punch quickly with both arms. Before every session: clear an area at least 2 × 2 metres (about 6½ × 6½ feet) around where you will play, with nothing within reach of your arms in front of you, beside you or above you, including furniture, walls, screens, lamps, ceiling fans and glass. Keep people and pets out of that space while you play, and make sure the floor is clear and not slippery. PowerPlay sends objects to your left and right hands one after the other, so you need the full width of that space on both sides.

Built rooms and your real room

In a built room the game replaces your view of the room you are really in. Apple Vision Pro fades your real surroundings back in if you move more than about 1.5 metres from where you started, and it may show them if you move quickly toward something. It does not warn you about furniture, walls or people within reach of your arms, and it does not watch your hands. So clear your space before you start, stay in one place, seated or standing, and do not walk around while you play. In your own room you see your real surroundings, but the same rules about space apply. The objects and pieces you see on your own tables, shelves and floor are not real: never reach for, lean on or step toward real furniture to hit one.

Your health

Warm up before you play and take regular breaks. Stop at once if you feel pain, dizziness, nausea, eye strain, disorientation or shortness of breath, and do not play again until you feel well. If you have a heart, joint, back or balance condition, are pregnant, or have any other medical concern, talk to a doctor before playing. The game contains flashes, bright breaking effects and fast motion; if you or anyone in your family has had seizures or blackouts, consult a doctor before playing. Read Apple's Important Safety Information for Apple Vision Pro and follow it.
